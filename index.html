Our Story

A home for the heritage of Asia, on the shores of the American West

Magnolia sits on 18 acres of wilderness near Fallen Leaf Lake, just outside South Lake Tahoe. We built this resort for the Asian American community and for anyone who loves Asia but doesn't have the time — or the miles — to cross an ocean to feel it.

Four buildings, each drawn from a different corner of Asia, surround a shared heart: gardens, water, mountain air, and the quiet architecture of belonging.

Stay

Rooms & Residences

Every building offers the same three tiers of comfort — the difference is which region of Asia welcomes you home.

Entry-Level Luxury

Standard Rooms

37–55 m² · 400–600 sq ft

A spacious sleeping zone paired with a large premium bathroom — often with a separate soaking tub and rain shower — plus a small indoor lounge or desk area.

Elevated

Luxury Suites

60–100+ m² · 650–1,000+ sq ft

A distinct, separate living room and private bedroom, enhanced closet space, and an upgraded balcony or terrace for slow mornings.

Signature

Resort Villas & Bungalows

100–250+ m² · 1,080–2,700+ sq ft

Expansive indoor-outdoor living with a private plunge pool or Jacuzzi, a sun deck, and dedicated outdoor seating among the pines.

Each of our four buildings — South, East, North & West — prices its rooms individually, so you can choose your region first and your room tier second.
